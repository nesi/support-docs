Subject: New rules for AI agents on Mahuika

Kia ora,

We're contacting you because you've used AI agents or coding assistants (such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or Copilot) on Mahuika.

To protect the shared system, REANNZ is introducing rules that AI agents must follow on Mahuika, this will be an ongoing project and we are open to your suggestions.

What you need to do
Log in to Mahuika and run this once on a login node:

/opt/nesi/share/mahuika-agents/install.sh

This points your agent at the REANNZ rules, so it reads them at the start of every session. It won't overwrite any of your files. If you use an agent on your own computer to run commands on Mahuika, the AI Agent Guidelines explain how to install the rules there.

What's changing and when

[DATE]: rules installed system-wide.
From [DATE]: if an agent is found breaking the rules, we may stop its processes or suspend access

Service accounts
If you need an agent to log in on its own, or to keep running while you aren't supervising it, it needs a service account. We're developing these now. Reply with your use case and requirements and we'll be in touch.
