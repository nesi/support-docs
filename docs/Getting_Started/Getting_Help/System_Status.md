## Check service status

[status.nesi.org.nz](https://status.nesi.org.nz) shows current incidents and scheduled maintenance for our services.
You can subscribe there to updates by email or text message.

## Manage your notifications

New users are subscribed to notifications for every service listed on status.nesi.org.nz, and can opt out.

To change which emails you get from us, either:

- log in to [my.nesi.org.nz](https://my.nesi.org.nz/account/preference), click **Account** in the left-side menu, then **My Preferences**.
    Use the **Manage** and **Subscribe** buttons to change your notifications about system status, newsletters and projects,
- or use the **Manage your subscription** or **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom of a notification email.
