Our support team may ask to review how efficiently your jobs run,
particularly if your project has, or is asking for, a large compute allocation.
Efficient jobs get you results sooner, and leave more room on the cluster for everyone.
To ask for a review yourself, {% include "partials/support_request.html" %}.

## What happens in a review

We spend some time (typically up to 10-20 hours) looking at your software and workflows,
and tell you what we find. A review ends in one of these outcomes:

- your jobs already run efficiently,
- we agree some improvements with you. We may be able to make quick wins within the review.
    For larger changes, we can help you apply for a [Consultancy](Consultancy.md) project.

## What we need from you

- Time to answer questions and share your code and data, by email or video meeting.
- Some test cases that represent your work but run faster,
    for example a simulation with the same domain size but fewer time steps.
